store blocks. Ladder Locker's capture also serves as the project-card thumbnail.

minor maintenance of ADO E-Bike's former Shopify store are based on the owner's
approved specification, not inferred from the screenshots. No ADO screenshot is
used. No newsletter signup or other form was submitted during capture.
